Meeting notes that never leave your iPhone.

Fieldnote records a meeting, transcribes it, works out who spoke when and writes the notes, all on the phone. No account, no server, no cloud model.

Join the TestFlight beta App Store release coming · iPhone 15 Pro and later, iOS 27

What stays on your phone

  • Audio, transcripts, speakers and notes. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account to upload to.
  • Every model runs on the phone: Apple's on-device models, or ones you download yourself.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app has no code that sends anything home, and its tests check that it stays that way.
  • Meeting content leaves only when you share it, or when you turn on Siri reading your notes. Read the privacy policy.

Record from anywhere

Start from the app, the Action button, Control Centre or Siri. A Live Activity shows the recording on the Lock Screen, and audio is saved as it goes, so a crash loses seconds, not the meeting.

Know who said what

Speakers are identified on the phone, line by line. Fieldnote picks up names from introductions like "Hi, I'm Priya", and you can rename anyone.

Notes you can check

An overview, topics, action items by owner, decisions and open questions. Every point links to the lines it came from; tap one to hear that moment.

Bring your own recordings

Import from Voice Memos, a dictaphone or a meeting service. Export as Markdown, text, PDF or subtitles, and send tasks to Reminders.

Choose your models

Apple's built-in speech and language models work out of the box. Optional downloads, such as NVIDIA's Parakeet and MiniCPM5, trade storage for accuracy.

Open source

The whole app is on GitHub under the BSD Zero Clause licence, privacy checks included.
