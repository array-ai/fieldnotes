Help with Fieldnote

Found a problem or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub. Please don't include recordings or transcripts.

Requirements

An iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 16 or later, on iOS 27 with Apple Intelligence turned on. Fieldnote relies on Apple's on-device models, which need that hardware.

Common questions

Why are the notes still being written after I left the app?

The transcript and speakers finish in the background. Apple's on-device model limits how much it writes for apps in the background, so the notes finish when you open Fieldnote again, or while the phone is charging if you turn on “Finish notes while charging”. Fieldnote notifies you when they're ready.

How do I try a different model on a meeting I already have?

Choose the model in Settings, open the meeting and use Redo to run the transcript, speakers or summary again. If you've edited the transcript, Fieldnote asks first, since a new transcript replaces your edits.

Does Fieldnote work offline?

Yes. Recording, transcription, speakers and notes all work in Airplane Mode. Only optional model downloads and Apple Maps place names need a connection.

Can Siri read my notes?

Only if you turn on “Let Siri read meeting notes” in Fieldnote's Settings. Then you can ask, for example, “Action items from my last Fieldnote meeting”. See the privacy policy for what Siri receives.

How do I free up storage?

Delete meetings you no longer need, or remove downloaded models in Settings → Models. Fieldnote keeps working with Apple's built-in models.

Reporting a bug

Turn on Debug mode in Settings to see the activity log. It records what ran, how long it took and what failed, never what was said. Include the relevant lines and your iPhone model in the issue.
